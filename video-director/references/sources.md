# Sources

This skill synthesizes the methodology of eight public repositories. Everything here is paraphrased; no reference file was copied. Each principle came from several projects reaching it independently. The full extraction, with the evidence per principle and the tensions between sources, is in `METHODOLOGY.md` alongside this skill's source.

| Repository | Main ideas drawn from it |
|---|---|
| github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos | Prompt levers that recur in strong prompts; failure patterns; how much iteration creators actually needed |
| github.com/tuzhechen2005/opus-video-skills | The reads model of viewer timing; acting principles; emphasis budget; restraint in sound |
| github.com/whaleyxbt/claude-motion | Numbers as deliberate defaults; a single timeline; contact sheets per beat; easing by role |
| github.com/howseen-ai/claude-motion-design | Harness over prompt; facts gate; signal-level QA (pops, flashes, loop seams); critics that default to reject |
| github.com/AbubakrChan/product-launch-motion | Slideshow vs screensaver; reveals cued to words and beats; truth whitelist; why a shorter skill worked better |
| github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar | Director stance; benchmark first; three structures; style invariants vs instances; perception as measurement |
| github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft | Rules as case law; energy arc; beat-grid fitting; independent clean-context review; perceptibility thresholds |
| github.com/cth9191/animate | Format grammar with coupling rules; story invariants; numeric arc checks; banned defaults |

One more source covers coded character and illustrated films: the maintainer's own production notes from about 30 projects (July–October 2026). It contributed the medium described as a process with texture bound to objects, the measurable arc, identity anchors and screen direction, turns, contacts, faces and hands, guards beside their beats, the visible-action check and divergence report, the fix card with its out-of-scope diff, certainty tags, and live pages. Its prompting of pixel video models is left to a separate skill.

Two practices found in the sources were deliberately left out: removing watermarks from AI-generated faces, and downloading third-party media with yt_dlp.
